# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Epora serves ordinary Cardano users who need shared control, daily spending limits, recurring payments, or key recovery without giving up custody. It also serves families, small teams, treasury operators, recovery contacts, payees, and Cardano builders evaluating permission rules. Users often review high-consequence transactions and need to understand who can act, what will change on-chain, and what remains protected.

## Product Purpose

Epora makes an on-chain permission wallet understandable and operable without requiring users to reason directly about validator internals. Success means a user can create or open a wallet, identify their role, choose the right action, preview the exact outcome, and sign with confidence. The current product is an experimental Cardano Preprod reference interface. It must never imply mainnet readiness, an audit, or custody of user keys.

## Brand Personality

Calm, protective, explicit. Epora should feel technically credible without sounding like protocol documentation. Copy is plain, concrete, and honest about risk. Visual emphasis helps users distinguish routine actions, irreversible changes, warnings, and on-chain status.

## Anti-references

Avoid speculative crypto aesthetics, neon-on-black spectacle, decorative dashboards, generic SaaS card grids, unexplained protocol jargon, raw provider errors, and copy that repeats a heading without helping the next decision. Avoid interfaces that make experimental software look production-certified or conceal risk behind reassuring decoration.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the consequence before asking for a signature.
2. Show the user's role, available actions, and blocked reasons in plain language.
3. Keep safety information persistent but proportional to the current decision.
4. Prefer one clear next action over an inventory of technical capabilities.
5. Make on-chain state, local state, loading, empty, error, and success states visually distinct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only operation, visible focus, semantic labels, screen-reader status announcements, zoom and reflow, reduced motion, increased contrast preferences, and non-color cues for every state. Use plain English and explain Cardano-specific terms at first use.
